🛠️ MAKER BUILD LOG & CASE STUDY

Achieving Zero Lacquer Fumes in an Apartment Room

How I engineered a modular 2020 aluminum airtight glove box enclosure around a standard portable desktop spray booth to permanently eliminate toxic fumes and keep my family happy.
STEP 1 • THE PROBLEM

The Fatal Flaw of Standard Open-Front Desktop Booths

Like many model makers, I bought a standard portable folding spray booth for airbrushing in my apartment room. However, its open-front design caused continuous problems whenever spraying lacquers or enamels:

  • Overspray Backdraft & Escape: Even with strong exhaust fans running, air turbulence from the airbrush rebound mist caused toxic paint particulates and lacquer thinner fumes to blow directly back into my room.
  • Hours of Exhausting Venting: A short 10-minute painting session required hours of room ventilation with all windows open in winter and summer.
❌ Severe Family Complaints: Toxic chemical smells spread across the whole house, causing headaches and intense family complaints. I was on the verge of being forced to abandon airbrushing completely.
STEP 2 • DESIGN CONCEPT

A Modular 2020 Aluminum Enclosure That Houses the Entire Booth

Buying a $500+ commercial industrial negative pressure cabinet wasn't an option. So I asked: "Why not build a secondary airtight chamber that completely encloses my existing portable booth, reusing its fan and lights?"

  • Rigid 2020 Aluminum Extrusions: Industrial AL6063-T5 aluminum framing with internal blind joints for a clean, non-sagging skeleton.
  • Spacious Chamber Dimensions: Sized at 800mm (W) x 600mm (D) x 500mm (H)—large enough to fit the entire booth, turntable, airbrush stand, and tall 1/35 scale or Gunpla PG models.
💡 ZERO Additional Fan Cost: Completely recycled the motor, filters, and LED lighting of my existing spray booth.
STEP 3 • FABRICATION

Precision Cutouts for Direct Exterior Duct Routing

The key challenge was routing the existing flexible exhaust duct through the enclosure wall straight out the window without letting fumes recirculate inside the room.

  • Duct Port Precision Hole: Hole-saw cut through the side/rear 6mm high-density MDF panel, matched tightly to the diameter of the flexible exhaust hose.
  • Dedicated DC Power Jack Pass-Through: Drilled a dedicated wire slot for the 12V power supply to maintain an airtight seal without pinching cables.
⚙️ True Negative Pressure: Fumes are pulled through the booth filter and expelled straight outdoors with zero leakage back into the living space.
STEP 4 • ASSEMBLY

Panels Slotting & Internal Booth Placement

Inserted the 6mm MDF baseboard and side panels into the 2020 profile extrusion slots, then placed the spray booth inside.

  • Vibration and Noise Dampening: The solid MDF floor and rigid extrusion frame significantly absorbed the fan motor's resonance and hum.
  • Optimized Ergonomics: Placed a rotating turntable right in front of the suction face, allowing effortless 360-degree rotation of scale models while spraying.
STEP 5 • 100% AIRTIGHT COMPLETED

Clear Acrylic Window & Dual 3D-Printed Glove Ports

Installed a 6.0mm high-transparency acrylic front window paired with custom **3D-printed Ø120mm dual glove ports** and elastic sealing sleeves.

  • 100% Optical Clarity: The crystal-clear front acrylic provides unobstructed, distortion-free viewing of the model under bright booth illumination.
  • Full Isolation Through Gloves: Hands slip directly into the sealed glove ports—airbrushing with zero exposure to skin, eyes, or lungs.
🎉 Real-World Outcome: Literally 0% thinner odor leaks into the room during or after spraying. Zero headaches, no family complaints, and complete creative freedom day or night!
